# 1718 CLUB

Premium digital loyalty and membership platform for 1718 Café.

## Product vision

1718 CLUB is designed as a premium membership experience rather than a discount card. The platform will support customer membership, loyalty, rewards, referrals, campaigns, staff operations, administration, analytics, and future POS/ordering integrations.

## Architecture principles

- Mobile-first customer experience
- Production-ready API-first architecture
- Immutable points ledger
- Configurable earning and reward rules
- Role-based staff/admin access
- Arabic/English with full RTL support
- Multi-location ready
- POS and ordering integration ready
- No secrets committed to source control

## Initial product surfaces

- Customer CLUB PWA
- Staff operations console
- Admin dashboard
- Loyalty engine
- Rewards engine
- CRM and analytics

## Initial loyalty configuration

The initial business configuration is intentionally configurable rather than hard-coded. The proposed starting rule is 1 EGP spent = 1 CLUB point, with configurable tiers, rewards, campaigns, expiry, and eligibility.

## Brand direction

Quiet luxury: deep charcoal, warm cream, walnut, restrained brass, elegant typography, generous whitespace, and premium coffee/tea imagery. The application should feel like a private members club, not a coupon app.
